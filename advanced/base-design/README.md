# Base Design
## General Tips and Guidelines
- Place your Aerial Defense Towers near your silos. ADT's are almost always catapulted first thing to allow healers free range without too much risk of dying quickly
- Place your Aerial Defense Towers away from each other so that they cannot both be taken out in the same 10 million pebble catapult
- Besides Aerial Defense Towers, you also kind of want to spread out other towers as well, to prevent the bulk of your defenses from being taken out with just a few catapults
- Keep in mind that you can be attacked 4 times without triggering damage protection if the damage is less than 25% total. This means you can be attacked up to 4 times with catapults easily
- When you level up your resource collectors to level 10, they can be used as meat shields. They have a large amount of health
- In Map Room 3, you want to protect your housings and spread them out. This is because if they are killed, your monsters in that housing are damaged to 50%. 
- If you want to be tricky, you can upgrade your hatcheries all the way. It gets very tall, and can obscure vision of the tower behind it. You can use this trick to hide your Aerial Defense Towers.
- Traps and heavy traps deal a LOT of damage, so you should make holes in your walls intentionally so that attacking monsters step into them.
